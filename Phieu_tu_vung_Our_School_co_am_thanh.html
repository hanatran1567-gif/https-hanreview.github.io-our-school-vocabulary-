1. Sentence Pattern

Is this our + place?
Yes, it is.
No, it isn't.

Ví dụ:

Is this our school? → Yes, it is.

Is this our library? → No, it isn't.

2. Fill in the blanks

  1. Look at the __________. (bức tranh)
  2. This is our __________. (trường học)
  3. This is our __________. We have many books here. (thư viện)
  4. We study in the __________. (lớp học)
  5. We play in the __________. (sân chơi)
